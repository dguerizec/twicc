---
title: "Terminal: tabs and persistent sessions"
---

The terminal is not limited to one shell. Use **+** to open more tabs
(double-click a tab to rename it), and attach shared terminals scoped to the
**Project**, **Workspace** or **Global** level — handy for long-running
commands you want reachable from any session.

Turn on **Settings → Terminal → Persistent sessions (tmux)** to keep your
terminals alive across reloads and reconnects; they are cleaned up when their
session is archived.
