---
title: "Search within a session: Ctrl+F"
platform: [desktop]
os: [linux, windows]
---

Press **Ctrl+F** — or click the magnifying-glass icon in the session header —
to find text in the current conversation. It replaces the browser's own Find;
press **Ctrl+F** again to fall back to it.

Use **F3** / **Shift+F3** to step through matches, **Esc** to close. To search
across every session, press **Ctrl+Shift+F**.
