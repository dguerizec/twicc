---
title: "Rearrange and save layouts"
platform: [desktop]
---

Each tab has a small chevron — use it to move Chat, Files, Git, Terminal or
Artifacts into a side or bottom dock, so you can watch several at once.

The overflow (⋮) menu at the right end of the tab bar saves your arrangement as
a named layout, reloads a saved one, or sets it as the default for new
sessions — globally or per project.
