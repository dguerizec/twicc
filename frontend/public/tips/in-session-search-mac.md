---
title: "Search within a session: Cmd+F"
platform: [desktop]
os: [mac]
---

Press **Cmd+F** — or click the magnifying-glass icon in the session header —
to find text in the current conversation. It replaces the browser's own Find;
press **Cmd+F** again to fall back to it.

Use **F3** / **Shift+F3** to step through matches, **Esc** to close. To search
across every session, press **Cmd+Shift+F**.
