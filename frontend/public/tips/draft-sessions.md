---
title: "Line up work with drafts"
---

Unsent sessions and messages are saved in your **browser** (not synced to your
other devices), so you can prepare several tasks ahead. Don't worry — they will
still be there after a reload, or even a restart of TwiCC.

Name each draft to tell them apart: the **pencil** in the session header, or
the **Rename** entry in its **⋮** menu in the sidebar.
