---
title: "Get notified when an agent is done"
---

TwiCC can alert you when an agent **finishes** or **needs your input**, even
when you are on another tab.

Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**, to pick a sound and/or a browser
notification per event. You can also add **push targets** (ntfy, Telegram,
Pushover and more) to get pinged on your phone — with an *away only* option
so you are only notified once you have stepped away.
