---
title: "Flip between recent sessions"
platform: [desktop]
---

Hold **Ctrl** and tap the key above **Tab** (`` ` `` on QWERTY keyboards) to
cycle through your recent sessions, Alt-Tab style — release **Ctrl** to land
on one. The up and down arrows work too while Ctrl is held.

Add **Shift** to cycle through the sessions currently shown in the sidebar
instead.
