---
title: "Keep an eye on your usage"
---

The rings at the bottom of the sidebar track how much of your provider's
quota you have used — over 5 hours, over 7 days, plus extra usage when it
kicks in.

Click a ring for the breakdown, including the current burn rate, plus a
**View graph** button charting usage and burn rate over time. **Settings →
Notifications** can even alert you the moment extra usage starts.
