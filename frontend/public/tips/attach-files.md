---
title: "Attach files by dropping them"
platform: [desktop]
---

Drag image or PDF (Claude only) files straight onto the chat to attach them,
or **paste** an image from your clipboard. Dragging plain text drops it into
the message at the cursor.

To send a file to a different session, drag it over that session in the
sidebar and hold for about a second until TwiCC switches to it — then drop it
onto the chat.
