---
title: "Edit and preview files"
---

In the **Files** tab you can open any file in the project and edit the
writable ones: click the pencil icon (or press **Alt+E**), make your change,
and save — without leaving TwiCC.

Many files also preview: toggle the eye icon to render Markdown, SVG, HTML or
Mermaid, and drag to pan or scroll to zoom on images.
