---
title: "Working with workspaces"
---

A workspace is a way to **group projects** together — purely for organization.
Say you keep a **backend** and a **frontend** in two separate git repos: put
them in one workspace to browse and work on them as a set. It doesn't move any
files or create a folder on disk; it's just a grouping inside TwiCC.

### What it does

Selecting a workspace **filters** your projects and sessions down to the ones it
contains, so you can focus on a subset of your work. Give it a color to spot it
at a glance.

### Auto-add patterns

Beyond adding projects by hand, a workspace can define **directory patterns**:
any newly-discovered project whose folder matches one joins the workspace
**automatically**, so you don't have to assign it each time.

### Good to know

A project can belong to **several workspaces at once** — or none. Workspaces
overlap freely; they're labels, not exclusive buckets.
